# Platform Governance Report

## Governance Readiness

Governance readiness reviews platform readiness, policy readiness, operational controls, and governance maturity.

## Operational Maturity

Operational maturity reviews process, testing, monitoring, and documentation maturity using configuration-driven levels.

## Risk Summary

Platform risk review classifies operational, dependency, governance, and sustainability risks as low, medium, high, or critical.

## Certification Summary

Governance certification aggregates governance readiness, operational maturity, platform risk, and sustainability readiness into certified, warning, or blocked states.

## Recommendations

- Keep governance reporting aggregate-only.
- Resolve critical platform risks before strategic expansion.
- Keep operations runbooks, tests, monitoring, and documentation aligned with production behavior.
- Avoid external governance, ERP, executive portal, or board-management integrations until a future explicit phase.
