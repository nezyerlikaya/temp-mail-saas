# Product Analytics Readiness Report

## Analytics Readiness

Analytics readiness reviews event readiness, aggregate metrics readiness, reporting readiness, and privacy readiness.

## Conversion Readiness

Conversion readiness reviews landing visits, mailbox creation, inbox activation, account registration, and premium conversion preparation.

## Retention Readiness

Retention readiness reviews revisit readiness, account retention readiness, premium retention readiness, and lifecycle readiness.

## Analytics Certification

Analytics certification aggregates analytics readiness, conversion readiness, journey readiness, and retention readiness into certified, warning, or blocked states.

## Recommendations

- Keep analytics configuration-driven.
- Use aggregate events only.
- Avoid external analytics providers until a future explicit phase.
- Do not store mailbox contents, email addresses, fingerprints, or personal profiles.
- Review conversion and retention signals before adding any tracking implementation.
