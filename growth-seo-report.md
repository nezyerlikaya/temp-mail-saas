# Growth & SEO Readiness Report

## SEO Readiness

SEO readiness now reviews sitemap availability, robots availability, structured data defaults, canonical behavior, and metadata defaults.

## Content Readiness

Content growth readiness reviews publication readiness, category readiness, tag readiness, SEO content fields, editorial readiness, and optional published content requirements.

## Indexing Readiness

Indexing readiness reviews sitemap coverage, robots coverage, canonical coverage, and crawl readiness without calling search engine APIs.

## Growth Certification

Growth certification aggregates SEO readiness, content readiness, indexing readiness, and landing page readiness into certified, warning, or blocked states.

## Recommendations

- Keep SEO checks configuration-driven.
- Publish content through controlled editorial workflows only.
- Avoid search engine API integrations until a future explicit step.
- Keep temporary inbox and mailbox-specific URLs excluded from organic discovery.
- Review sitemap and robots output before public content expansion.
