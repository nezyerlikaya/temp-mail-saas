# STEP44 Public Beta Readiness Report

## Beta Readiness

STEP44 adds a controlled public beta readiness layer. It reviews onboarding, support, feedback, monitoring, incident readiness, and RC3 certification without launching publicly.

## Blockers

Blockers include incomplete installer flow, missing registration/login/inbox flows, missing active onboarding domain, incompatible provider readiness, critical incidents, and failed RC3 certification.

## Warnings

Warnings include missing localization support, disabled monitoring, incomplete support runbooks, missing escalation paths, and incomplete feedback/issue intake documentation.

## Recommendations

- Resolve blockers before opening beta access.
- Assign support owners for accepted warnings.
- Keep feedback intake manual until an external system is approved.
- Re-run public beta status after RC3 or operational config changes.

## Certification Summary

`system:public-beta-status` reports certified, warning, or blocked status with safe blocker, warning, and recommendation counts.

## Launch Guidance

This step does not launch the product, enable marketing systems, integrate ticketing, activate payments, or send public traffic. After approval, proceed to STEP45 Production Launch v1.0.0.
