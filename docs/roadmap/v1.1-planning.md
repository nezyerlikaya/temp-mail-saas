# v1.1 Roadmap Planning

STEP55 prepares long-term maintenance and evolution without implementing customer-facing features or large refactors.

## Technical Debt Inventory

- Review repeated readiness result formatting for a future shared helper.
- Review public content route activation before editorial expansion.
- Design aggregate-only product analytics storage before collecting conversion metrics.
- Classify each item by severity, remediation priority, and risk level.

## Architecture Review Summary

- Keep module boundaries documented.
- Review service responsibilities before adding abstractions.
- Review dependency structure when adding new integrations.
- Preserve backward compatibility across inbox, auth, RBAC, installer, and operations flows.

## Maintainability Summary

- Keep tests close to the readiness service they protect.
- Keep deployment and operator documentation current.
- Review code organization before consolidating repeated patterns.
- Keep operational continuity visible through safe events.

## Scalability Summary

- Review queue scalability before traffic expansion.
- Review provider and domain scalability together.
- Review operations visibility before increasing workload.
- Review billing scalability before activating a real provider.

## Roadmap Candidates

- v1.1: readiness consolidation review, aggregate-only analytics design, and runbook maintenance cadence.
- v1.2: editorial content route planning and billing provider activation planning.
- Future backlog: enterprise controls review and persistent inbox ownership review.

## Prioritization Methodology

Prioritize stability, privacy, backward compatibility, and operational continuity. Treat critical debt as a blocker, high debt as a warning, and medium or low debt as tracked planning input.
