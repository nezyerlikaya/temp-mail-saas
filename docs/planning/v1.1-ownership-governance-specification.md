# v1.1 Ownership Governance Specification

## Purpose

Ownership governance defines who owns, controls, reviews, and escalates future billing and commercial resources. This is planning-only.

## Ownership Types

Future ownership types should include personal ownership, shared ownership, organization ownership, enterprise ownership, and managed ownership.

## Ownership Responsibilities

Each ownership type should define:

- Owner: accountable person or entity.
- Controller: actor allowed to manage the resource.
- Reviewer: actor responsible for governance review.
- Escalation owner: actor responsible when ownership is unclear or disputed.

## Billing Ownership

Future billing ownership should support billing owner, billing administrator, finance reviewer, procurement reviewer, auditor, organization owner, and contract manager roles.

## Escalation Model

Ownership conflicts should escalate from billing administrator to billing owner, then to organization owner, then to enterprise operations for managed accounts.

## Audit Model

Ownership changes should always produce future audit evidence covering previous owner, new owner, requester, approver, scope, reason, and effective date.

## Non-Implementation Boundary

No ownership enforcement, permission change, role change, organization model, or authorization behavior is implemented by this step.
