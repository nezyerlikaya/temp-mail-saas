# v1.1 Enterprise Contract Model

## Purpose

This document specifies the future enterprise contract model for Temp Mail SaaS v1.1 planning. It does not create contracts, billing records, legal workflows, or procurement integrations.

## Contract Lifecycle

Recommended future states are draft, approved, active, renewal review, suspended, terminated, and archived.

## Contract Renewal

Renewal review should consider usage, seats, support activity, security posture, domain readiness, provider reliability, compliance evidence, cost trend, and procurement requirements.

## Contract Suspension

Suspension should preserve ownership, audit evidence, export rights, retention obligations, and support escalation while limiting future access according to policy.

## Contract Termination

Termination should define export rights, data retention, seat revocation, domain handling, API key handling, billing closure, and final evidence archive.

## Contract Transfer

Contract transfer should require owner approval, finance review, procurement review, audit evidence, and clear effective date.

## Contract Ownership

Future contract ownership should define enterprise sponsor, billing owner, procurement reviewer, finance reviewer, contract manager, support owner, and escalation owner.

## Non-Implementation Boundary

No contract storage, workflow engine, legal document handling, external procurement integration, or enterprise billing automation is implemented in STEP74.
