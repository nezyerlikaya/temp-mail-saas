# v1.1 Organization Onboarding and Offboarding

## Scope

This document specifies future organization lifecycle models only. STEP71 does not implement organization provisioning, billing changes, exports, retention workflows, or audit events.

## Onboarding Models

### Self-Service Organization

Customer-led setup with safe defaults and limited enterprise controls.

### Assisted Organization

Guided setup by support or customer success with governance checklist.

### Enterprise Contract Organization

Contract-backed setup with security, compliance, billing, and identity review.

## Offboarding Models

| Type | Data Ownership | Export Right | Retention Effect | Audit Required |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Voluntary Closure | Organization owned | Yes | Standard retention | Yes |
| Contract Expiration | Organization owned | Yes | Contract retention | Yes |
| Security Suspension | Organization owned | Restricted | Security hold | Yes |
| Compliance Closure | Organization owned | Approved only | Compliance hold | Yes |
| Forced Termination | Organization owned | Restricted | Legal or policy review | Yes |

## Future Guardrails

- Offboarding should preserve audit evidence.
- Export rights should be policy-scoped.
- Security suspension should not silently purge data.
- Contract closure should separate billing termination from data lifecycle.
