# v1.1 Enterprise Gap Analysis

## Critical Gaps

- Organization entity and membership lifecycle are not implemented.
- Workspace, department, and team boundaries are not implemented.
- Delegated organization administration is not implemented.
- Organization billing ownership and seat lifecycle are not implemented.
- Tenant isolation strategy is not implemented.
- Enterprise SSO, MFA policy, access reviews, and organization audit trails are not implemented.

## Tenancy Analysis

### Single Database

Advantages: low operational cost, simple maintenance, low migration complexity.

Disadvantages: weak isolation, hard enterprise story, greater risk if organization scoping becomes complex.

Temp Mail SaaS fit: good short-term for personal and early team accounts only.

### Shared Database

Advantages: moderate cost, scalable enough for many SaaS products, simplest path to organization_id scoping.

Disadvantages: requires disciplined query scoping, policy enforcement, and testing.

Temp Mail SaaS fit: recommended starting point for future organization support.

### Schema Per Tenant

Advantages: stronger isolation and clearer enterprise separation.

Disadvantages: high migration complexity, higher operational load, more difficult support workflows.

Temp Mail SaaS fit: selective enterprise option only after revenue demand is proven.

### Database Per Tenant

Advantages: strongest isolation, best for strict enterprise contracts.

Disadvantages: very high operational cost, migration complexity, backup complexity, and maintenance burden.

Temp Mail SaaS fit: enterprise-only path, not appropriate for first organization release.
