# v1.1 SCIM Readiness Analysis

## Scope

This document is analysis-only. STEP71 does not implement SCIM, automated provisioning, federated provisioning, or deprovisioning.

## SCIM Areas

### SCIM User Lifecycle

Future standardized create, update, suspend, disable, and delete identity lifecycle.

### Group Lifecycle

Future group creation, update, membership, and removal lifecycle.

### Membership Sync

Future mapping from identity provider groups to organization, workspace, team, and roles.

### Deprovisioning

Future automatic access revocation and lifecycle audit safety.

### Attribute Mapping

Future mapping for email, name, external id, status, department, and manager.

### Role Mapping

Future bounded mapping from identity provider groups or attributes to internal roles.

## Provisioning Risks

- Over-Provisioning Risk
- Under-Provisioning Risk
- Privilege Escalation Risk
- Stale Access Risk
- Orphaned Identity Risk
- Automation Failure Risk

Each risk should include detection, governance requirement, and audit requirement in future implementation.
