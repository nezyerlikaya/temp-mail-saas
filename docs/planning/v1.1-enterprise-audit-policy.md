# v1.1 Enterprise Audit Policy

## Scope

This document specifies future enterprise audit access. STEP68 does not create audit exports, archive jobs, purge workflows, event models, or queue jobs.

## Audit Access Model

### Who Can Read

- Owner
- Organization Admin
- Security Reviewer
- Compliance Reviewer
- External Auditor

### Who Can Export

- Owner
- Compliance Reviewer

### Who Can Archive

- Owner
- Organization Admin

### Who Can Purge

- Owner

## Data Export Policy

### User Export

Personal data and user-owned resource export.

### Organization Export

Organization-owned operational data export.

### Compliance Export

Policy-approved compliance evidence export.

### Audit Export

Audit log export with approval and retention controls.

## Safety Requirements

- Export must be scoped.
- Export must be auditable.
- Emergency access must expire.
- Purge must be restricted.
- Audit visibility must be separated from audit mutation.
