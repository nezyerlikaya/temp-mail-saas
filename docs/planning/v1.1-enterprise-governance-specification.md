# v1.1 Enterprise Governance Specification

## Scope

STEP69 is specification-only. It does not implement migrations, models, policy engines, compliance engines, tenant systems, organization systems, auth changes, billing changes, permission changes, mailbox changes, queues, or events.

## Governance Domains

| Domain | Purpose | Scope | Future Owner Role | Risk | Priority |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Identity Governance | Control enterprise identity ownership and lifecycle | Users, members, delegated roles | Organization Owner | High | High |
| Access Governance | Review and control access grants | Roles, resources, delegated access | Security Reviewer | High | High |
| Data Governance | Classify, retain, export, and delete data safely | Mailboxes, messages, reports, logs | Compliance Reviewer | High | High |
| Security Governance | Govern security posture and incident response | Sessions, devices, MFA, SSO, events | Security Reviewer | High | High |
| Audit Governance | Ensure audit trails are complete and exportable | Security, billing, API, policy, access changes | Compliance Reviewer | Medium | High |
| Compliance Governance | Prepare evidence and control coverage | GDPR, SOC 2, ISO 27001, contracts | Organization Owner | High | Medium |
| Retention Governance | Control retention and legal hold readiness | Mail data, audit logs, billing metadata, API logs | Compliance Reviewer | Medium | Medium |
| Export Governance | Control exports and evidence records | Exports, approvals, evidence | Compliance Reviewer | Medium | Medium |
| Incident Governance | Classify and manage enterprise incidents | Security, abuse, data, API, billing, provider incidents | Security Reviewer | High | High |
