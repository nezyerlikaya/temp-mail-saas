# v1.1 Identity Risk Model

## Scope

This document specifies future identity risk categories only. It does not implement detection, scoring, automation, queues, or events.

## Risk Categories

| Risk | Description | Example Signals | Audit Required | Suggested Action |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Credential Risk | Compromised or weak credential risk | Failed logins, password reuse, breach indicator | Yes | Force reset and review access |
| Session Risk | Suspicious or stale session risk | New location, high privilege session, long-lived session | Yes | Revoke session and require MFA |
| Device Risk | Unknown or unsafe device risk | Unmanaged, revoked, risky device | Yes | Block or step-up authentication |
| Federation Risk | Identity provider trust or mapping risk | Metadata expiry, claim mismatch, issuer change | Yes | Pause federation and review trust |
| Insider Risk | Authorized user misuse risk | Bulk export, privilege change, unusual access | Yes | Open access review |
| Privilege Risk | Excessive permission or role drift | Stale admin, unreviewed role, emergency access | Yes | Certify or revoke access |
| API Identity Risk | Service or API identity misuse | Key spike, old key, unowned key | Yes | Rotate key and review owner |

## Future Guardrails

- Identity risk must be auditable.
- Emergency access must expire.
- Service identities must have owners.
- Federation mapping must be reviewable.
- Session and device risk should support future step-up authentication.
