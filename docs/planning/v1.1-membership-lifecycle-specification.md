# v1.1 Membership Lifecycle Specification

## Scope

This document specifies future membership and seat lifecycle behavior only. No models, migrations, roles, permissions, invitations, or team systems are implemented in STEP67.

## Membership Domain

Future entity: `Membership`

Purpose: connect a user to an organization, workspace, team, and role boundary.

## Membership States

### Invited

The organization has invited a user. No full access should be granted until the invitation is accepted and validated.

### Pending

The invitee has started onboarding or verification. Access should remain limited.

### Active

The member has usable organization access within assigned scopes.

### Suspended

The member is temporarily blocked but not removed from historical records or billing review.

### Removed

The member no longer has organization access. Historical audit references should remain.

## Suggested Transitions

- Invited to Pending
- Pending to Active
- Active to Suspended
- Suspended to Active
- Active to Removed
- Invited to Removed
- Pending to Removed

## Seat Lifecycle

### Assigned Seat

Seat is attached to an active or pending member.

### Unassigned Seat

Seat is available for assignment.

### Reserved Seat

Seat is held for hiring, contractor onboarding, or planned expansion.

### Suspended Seat

Seat is blocked from use while retaining billing or compliance context.

## Role Boundaries

- Organization Owner controls ownership and final authority.
- Organization Admin manages daily administration.
- Workspace Admin manages scoped workspace responsibilities.
- Team Lead manages team coordination.
- Member receives standard access.
- Guest receives restricted access.

## Safety Notes

Future implementation must avoid changing the existing user model directly. Membership should be additive and scoped.
