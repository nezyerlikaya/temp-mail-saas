# v1.1 Compliance Evidence Readiness

## Purpose

This document specifies future compliance evidence readiness for enterprise customers. It is not a compliance implementation and does not create data stores, workflows, exports, or certifications.

## Evidence Families

Future compliance evidence should be grouped into GDPR evidence, SOC 2 evidence, ISO 27001 evidence, and enterprise contract evidence. Each evidence family should define scope, retention, ownership, review cadence, export rules, and approval rules.

## GDPR Readiness

GDPR evidence should eventually prove readiness for access requests, deletion requests, export requests, retention controls, processing governance, and auditability.

## SOC 2 Readiness

SOC 2 evidence should eventually support security, availability, confidentiality, change management, incident response, access governance, and monitoring controls.

## ISO 27001 Readiness

ISO 27001 evidence should eventually support risk treatment, asset ownership, access control, incident management, supplier risk, operational controls, and audit review.

## Enterprise Contract Readiness

Enterprise contract evidence should eventually support customer-specific retention commitments, support obligations, security controls, review cadence, and export boundaries.

## Evidence Safety Rules

Evidence packages should be least-detail, scoped, approved, and sanitized. They should not contain mailbox contents, raw webhook payloads, secrets, provider credentials, DNS credentials, private keys, or unrestricted personal data.

## Current Gap

The platform has strong readiness reporting patterns, but future compliance readiness needs governed evidence storage, approval workflows, retention rules, export policy, and customer-specific evidence packages.
