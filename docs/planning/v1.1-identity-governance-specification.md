# v1.1 Identity Governance Specification

## Scope

STEP70 is specification-only. It does not implement SAML, OIDC, OAuth federation, MFA, auth changes, user model changes, permission changes, billing changes, tenant systems, migrations, queues, events, or data writes.

## Identity Sources

### Local Identity

Application-owned identity for current users and personal accounts.

### Organization Identity

Future organization-owned identity relationship for enterprise accounts.

### Federated Identity

Future identity delegated to SAML, OIDC, or OAuth federation provider.

### Service Identity

Future non-human identity for service-to-service and automation workflows.

## Identity Lifecycle

Future states:

- Invited
- Pending Activation
- Active
- Suspended
- Locked
- Disabled
- Archived

Suggested transitions:

- Invited to Pending Activation
- Pending Activation to Active
- Active to Suspended
- Suspended to Active
- Active to Locked
- Locked to Active
- Active to Disabled
- Disabled to Archived
