# Enterprise Readiness & Account Management

STEP58 strengthens enterprise account foundations without SSO, SCIM, identity federation, or an enterprise portal.

## Enterprise Readiness Model

- Enterprise account health reviews organization activity, membership activity, billing issues, support activity, and operational risks.
- Health states are `healthy`, `attention`, and `risk`.
- Certification aggregates account health, governance, lifecycle, and membership readiness.

## Governance Model

- Review aggregate role distribution.
- Review permission configuration readiness.
- Review organization ownership coverage.
- Keep governance output aggregate-only.

## Account Lifecycle Model

- Review onboarding readiness.
- Review growth readiness.
- Review billing readiness.
- Review suspension readiness.
- Review recovery readiness.

## Membership Intelligence Model

- Review recent membership growth.
- Review inactive membership trends.
- Review status distribution.
- Generate organization health insights without exposing member lists.

## Privacy Protections

- Do not expose user lists.
- Do not expose member details.
- Do not expose emails, names, tenant membership records, or identity provider data.
- Keep operations events aggregate-only.
- Keep SAML, OIDC, SCIM, and external identity providers disabled.
