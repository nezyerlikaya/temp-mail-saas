# Growth & SEO Readiness

STEP53 prepares organic growth without generating content, integrating analytics, or connecting to search engine APIs.

## SEO Checklist

- Confirm shared metadata defaults are configured.
- Confirm canonical URLs ignore transient query parameters.
- Confirm Open Graph and Twitter defaults remain centralized in the SEO service.
- Keep SEO output public-safe and free of credentials, tokens, or provider data.

## Sitemap Checklist

- Confirm `/sitemap.xml` is available.
- Confirm configured static pages are routable.
- Confirm published content can be included when content routes are introduced.
- Keep temporary inbox URLs and session-specific mailbox URLs out of the sitemap.

## Robots Checklist

- Confirm `/robots.txt` is available.
- Confirm production allows crawling only when intended.
- Confirm non-production environments remain conservative.
- Include the sitemap reference when sitemap support is enabled.

## Structured Data Checklist

- Confirm WebSite structured data is available.
- Confirm Organization structured data is available.
- Confirm future content pages can use Article structured data.
- Do not generate spam-like structured data or fabricated content signals.

## Content Checklist

- Confirm the content table supports publishing fields.
- Confirm SEO title and SEO description fields are available.
- Confirm category, tag, and editorial workflows are documented before content scaling.
- Do not generate AI content or publish pages automatically in this phase.

## Indexing Checklist

- Confirm sitemap, robots, canonical, and crawl readiness checks are green.
- Confirm launch pages are discoverable through the sitemap.
- Confirm no search engine API submission is required.
- Treat indexing as a readiness review, not a traffic generation workflow.
