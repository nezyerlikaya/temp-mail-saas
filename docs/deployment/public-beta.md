# Public Beta Launch Preparation

## Beta Launch Checklist

- Confirm RC3 certification is green or explicitly accepted.
- Confirm installer, registration, login, inbox, localization, domain, and provider flows.
- Confirm public beta access is controlled and reversible.
- Do not activate paid payment flows for beta.

## Onboarding Checklist

- Installer is complete and locked.
- Registration and login routes are available.
- Public inbox generation, polling, and message detail routes are available.
- Localization switch flow is available.
- At least one active onboarded domain and compatible provider are ready.

## Support Checklist

- Incident process is available.
- Monitoring process is enabled.
- Public beta runbook is documented.
- Escalation paths and troubleshooting guidance are documented.
- Issue triage owners are clear.

## Issue Triage Checklist

- Severity: critical, high, medium, low.
- Owners: platform, provider, domain, billing, operations, support.
- Critical issues pause beta expansion until reviewed.

## Monitoring Checklist

- Review operations dashboard.
- Review active alerts and incidents.
- Confirm support owners can see blockers and warnings.

## Rollback Reminders

- Keep rollback readiness current.
- Suspend beta access before risky operational changes.
- Keep provider, domain, and queue rollback paths documented.
