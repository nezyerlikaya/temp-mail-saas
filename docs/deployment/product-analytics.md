# Product Analytics Readiness

STEP54 prepares product analytics and conversion intelligence without external analytics providers, personal data profiling, fingerprinting, or mailbox content collection.

## Analytics Checklist

- Confirm analytics readiness events can be recorded through operations events.
- Confirm aggregate metrics foundations are available.
- Confirm reporting readiness is documented.
- Keep analytics output aggregate and operational.

## Privacy Checklist

- Do not collect mailbox contents.
- Do not collect email addresses.
- Do not create personal behavior profiles.
- Do not use device fingerprinting.
- Do not enable external analytics providers in this phase.

## Conversion Checklist

- Review landing visit readiness.
- Review mailbox creation readiness.
- Review inbox activation readiness.
- Review account registration readiness.
- Review premium conversion readiness without payment processing.

## Retention Checklist

- Review revisit readiness.
- Review account retention readiness.
- Review premium retention readiness.
- Review lifecycle readiness using existing retention foundations.

## Reporting Checklist

- Keep reports safe for operators.
- Show blockers, warnings, certification state, and recommendations.
- Exclude raw user identifiers, mailbox addresses, email content, tokens, and secrets.
