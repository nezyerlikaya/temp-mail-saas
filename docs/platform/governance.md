# Platform Governance & Strategic Operations

STEP60 creates a strategic stewardship foundation without ERP systems, board-management tools, executive dashboards, or external governance platforms.

## Governance Model

- Review platform readiness.
- Review policy readiness.
- Review operational controls.
- Review governance maturity.
- Classify governance state as healthy, attention, or risk.

## Maturity Model

- Review process maturity.
- Review testing maturity.
- Review monitoring maturity.
- Review documentation maturity.
- Stable and improving maturity are accepted; developing maturity is a warning; unknown values are blocked.

## Risk Model

- Review operational risk.
- Review dependency risk.
- Review governance risk.
- Review sustainability risk.
- Risk levels are low, medium, high, and critical.

## Certification Model

- Certify governance readiness.
- Certify operational maturity.
- Certify risk readiness.
- Certify sustainability readiness.
- Certification states are certified, warning, and blocked.

## Privacy Protections

- Governance reports are aggregate-only.
- No sensitive operational payloads are stored.
- No board, ERP, or compliance platform credentials are stored.
- No external governance integrations are invoked.
