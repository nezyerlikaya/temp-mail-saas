# Enterprise Readiness Report

## Enterprise Readiness

Enterprise account health now reviews organization activity, membership activity, billing issues, support issues, and operational risk counts.

## Governance Readiness

Governance readiness reviews aggregate role distribution, permission configuration, ownership coverage, and governance risks.

## Lifecycle Readiness

Organization lifecycle readiness covers onboarding, growth, billing, suspension, and recovery review points.

## Certification Summary

Enterprise certification aggregates account health, governance, lifecycle, and membership intelligence into certified, warning, or blocked states.

## Recommendations

- Keep enterprise reporting aggregate-only.
- Review inactive memberships and ownerless organizations before expanding enterprise capabilities.
- Keep external identity providers out of scope until an explicit future phase.
- Preserve existing tenant-context, organization membership, RBAC, and billing foundations.
