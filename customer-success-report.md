# Customer Success Report

## Support Readiness

First-party support request storage is available with optional user and organization ownership, enum-backed classification, lifecycle timestamps, privacy-safe text handling, and sanitized metadata.

## Customer Health Readiness

Customer health scoring reviews aggregate support activity, feedback activity, billing issues, abuse issues, and operational risk signals. The health state is classified as healthy, attention, or risk.

## Support Metrics Readiness

Support analytics reports aggregate response time, resolution time, open request count, category distribution, priority distribution, and status distribution.

## Recommendations

- Keep support reports aggregate-only.
- Review recurring themes during product planning.
- Escalate unresolved high-priority support requests for retention review.
- Keep external helpdesk integrations disabled until an explicit future phase.
- Never expose ticket text, email addresses, mailbox contents, payloads, or request bodies in events or operator summaries.
