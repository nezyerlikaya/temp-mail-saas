# Product Intelligence Report

## Feedback Foundation

First-party feedback storage is available with optional user ownership, enum-backed classification, privacy-safe text handling, and sanitized metadata.

## Feedback Aggregation

Feedback can be aggregated by type, category, priority, and status without exposing user messages.

## Product Intelligence

Product intelligence identifies category trends, recurring issue categories, feature request demand, and roadmap recommendations using aggregate counts only.

## Roadmap Insights

Roadmap insight generation converts feature requests and suggestions into category-based candidates with low, medium, or high demand levels. High-priority issues are summarized as roadmap risks.

## Privacy Guidance

- Do not expose feedback messages in reports.
- Do not store secrets, tokens, mailbox contents, raw payloads, or email fields in metadata.
- Keep external feedback platforms disabled until an explicit future phase.
