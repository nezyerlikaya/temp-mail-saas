# v1.1 Feature Candidates Report

## Candidate Summary

The v1.1 feature candidate registry is available with enum-backed category, priority, status, effort, and risk classification.

## Prioritization Summary

Prioritization compares impact and priority against effort and risk. The result is an aggregate score and a recommendation to prioritize or review.

## Quick Wins

Quick wins are accepted candidates with strong scores and low risk.

## Risks

High-risk and critical-risk candidates are separated for explicit implementation readiness review.

## Recommended v1.1 Release Plan

- Start with accepted low-risk quick wins.
- Keep high-risk items out of the first implementation phase until reviewed.
- Keep deferred items visible but outside active v1.1 implementation.
- Do not implement any v1.1 feature until a dedicated feature step starts.
